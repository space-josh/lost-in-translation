# Commander Acorn

Commander Acorn is the face of the presentation.

He is an engineer.

An observer.

A communicator.

He notices signals.

He asks questions.

He improves understanding.

He is not a mascot.

He is not comic relief.

He represents the audience.

# Color Palette

Use the Brand Lab Visual Essence tokens in `styles.css` as the source of truth.

Core surfaces

- Ink 900 — #131216
- Ink 700 — #1D1C22
- Ink 500 — #2A2830
- Cream 50 — #F7F2EA
- Cream 100 — #EFE6D5
- Cream 200 — #E6D9C1

Brand accent

- Purple 700 — #561A8B
- Purple 500 — #7B25C6
- Purple 300 — #9B59D4
- Purple 100 — #D7BEEE

Utility accents

- Terracotta 500 — #D86D4A
- Terracotta 300 — #E19075
- Sage — #A9A996
- Rust — #692818
- Olive — #59564A

Use purple sparingly for signal, progress, understanding, and momentum. Use
terracotta for incomplete or caution states. Use sage and olive for ready or
successful states. Do not introduce alternate blue, green, or amber accents.

# Typography

Display

Clash Grotesk

Body

Spline Sans

Labels and telemetry

Spline Sans Mono

All three typefaces are self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` for offline use.

Hierarchy is large.

Confident.

Readable from 20 feet.

Never use tiny explanatory text.

---

# Illustration Style

Realistic CGI.

Photoreal.

Engineering.

No cartoons.

No clip art.

No emoji.

Commander Acorn must remain visually consistent.

---

# Commander Acorn Rules

Never crop awkwardly.

Never stretch.

Never distort.

Never use AI generations that change his proportions.

Preferred poses:

Holding tablet

Observing

Reviewing screens

Communicating

Thinking

Walking

Avoid exaggerated emotion.

# Layout Principles

Large margins.

One dominant focal point.

Readable in a large conference room.

Visual rhythm over density.

Every slide should breathe.

---

# Photography

Only when needed.

Preference is rendered environments.

Earth orbit

Mission control

Docking station

Cargo spacecraft

Engineering interiors

---

# Artwork Usage

Artwork supports story.

It never decorates empty space.

Commander Acorn appears intentionally.

A little delight.

Never overload.

---

# Integrate Branding

Use official SVG assets.

Do not recreate the logo.

Do not recolor the logo.

Brand presence should feel understated.

The presentation belongs to Integrate,
not to Mission Alpha.

Mission Alpha is the narrative wrapper.

---

# Overall Feeling

If someone described the presentation afterwards they should say:

"It felt like navigating a beautifully designed mission control system."

Not:

"It had cool squirrel graphics."

The engineering professionalism always comes first.

The delight comes second
.
