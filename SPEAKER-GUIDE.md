# Lost in Translation — Speaker Guide

Target duration: 25–30 minutes. Keynote content: 17–19 minutes. Workshop and debrief: 8–11 minutes.

## Controls
- Right arrow / Space: reveal or advance
- Left arrow: go back
- F: fullscreen
- Click: advance (except timer button)

## Scene beats
1. **Title:** Let the image breathe. “Every mission begins with someone noticing something.”
2. **Incoming transmission:** Establish that the signal is technically correct but incomplete for the audience.
3. **Translation:** Ask the room what changed before revealing the answer.
4. **Every role sees a signal:** Name each internal role and emphasize that no one role owns the whole picture.
5. **Merchandising:** Explain that limited capacity makes awareness and value translation important.
6. **Signal to value:** Use the prompt: “Because we do this, what becomes easier, safer, faster, clearer, or more valuable?”
7. **Four Ps:** Signal is the foundation; the Four Ps organize the translation.
8. **Product:** The mechanism is implementation; mission time is value.
9. **Place:** Same truth, different emphasis for different audiences.
10. **Price:** State the investment and tradeoff directly.
11. **Promotion:** Connect repetition to recognition, understanding, alignment, and momentum.
12. **Toolkit:** Emphasize that this can produce a two-sentence message, not paperwork.
13. **Before/after:** Merchandising does not guarantee acceptance; it improves the discussion.
14. **Workshop:** Ask participants to choose real work, not a fictional prompt.
15. **Canvas:** Run five minutes. Circulate and coach toward outcomes and concrete asks.
16. **Debrief:** Ask, “Who discovered something about their own idea?”
17. **Shared language:** Keep this brief; consider the question aloud.
18. **Close:** Slow down. Deliver the last two lines and stop.
