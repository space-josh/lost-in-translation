# DECISIONS.md

## ADR-001
Presentations are built in HTML, not PowerPoint.

Reason:
Treat the presentation like a product. Enables richer layouts, motion, offline portability, and reusable components.

---

## ADR-002
Commander Acorn is a narrative guide, not a mascot.

Reason:
Supports the audience without distracting from the teaching.

---

## ADR-003
The Docking Sequence is the canonical metaphor.

Reason:
One repeated metaphor is more memorable than many different ones.

---

## ADR-004
The Four Ps always begin with a Problem.

Reason:
Merchandising starts with recognizing an opportunity, not proposing a solution.

---

## ADR-005
Momentum is earned through clarity and repetition, not volume.

Reason:
Reinforces one of the core behavioral changes the presentation seeks to create.

---

## ADR-006
Mission Alpha is an internal codename.

Reason:
It should never appear in the audience-facing presentation.

---

## ADR-007
Commander Acorn artwork must remain visually consistent.

Reason:
Avoids the uncanny inconsistency common in AI-generated characters and reinforces a recognizable narrative identity.

---

## ADR-008
Visuals support the story; they never compete with it.

Reason:
Every illustration should earn its place and advance the audience's understanding.
